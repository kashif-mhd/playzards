Delete your data

For Octamine by Playzards

Octamine doesn't need an account, and you never give us your name or email address to play. Here's how to delete what the game keeps.

On your phone

Your progress, stars, coins, power-ups, lives, settings and any unfinished level are stored only on your phone.

  • Android: uninstall Octamine, or open Settings → Apps → Octamine → Storage and tap Clear storage to start over.
  • iPhone: touch and hold the Octamine icon, then tap Remove App → Delete App.

This deletes everything at once and can't be undone.

Online (cloud save and leaderboards)

If you've used Octamine's optional online features, a copy of your progress, your leaderboard name, scores and country is kept with our service provider, Unity Gaming Services, under a player ID created for the game.

To delete it, email us at playzardsgames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Octamine data" and your leaderboard name. We'll delete your player ID, cloud save and leaderboard entries within 30 days and reply to confirm. We don't keep any of it afterwards, unless the law requires us to.

Google Play Games and Game Center

If you signed in with Google Play Games or Game Center, that profile belongs to Google or Apple. You can manage or delete it in the Play Games app or in your iPhone's Game Center settings.

Ads

If you've seen ads in the game, you can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in its settings at any time. See our Privacy Policy for more.
