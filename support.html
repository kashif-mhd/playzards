Playing

How do I play?

Each number shows how many mines touch that cell. An octagon touches 8 cells and a diamond touches 4. Tap to open a cell, hold to place a flag, and tap a number whose flags are all placed to open everything else around it. The flag button at the bottom switches what a tap does: open, flag, or mark "?" on a cell you're not sure about yet.

Do I ever have to guess?

Never. Every level is checked by a solver, so it can be cleared by logic alone. If you're stuck, there's always a cell you can prove is safe. A Hint will show you one.

What are shields?

Each level gives you 3 shields. Hitting a mine breaks one. If you lose all of them, the level is lost, but you can keep going with an extra Shield.

Lives, coins and power-ups

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. Losing a level costs one, and so does restarting or abandoning a level after you've hit a mine. A life comes back every 30 minutes; the timer is next to the hearts. Out of lives? Wait for the next one, or buy one with coins in the market.

How do I get coins and power-ups?

Clearing a level for the first time earns coins: more for more stars. Every first clear also fills the gift bar, and a full bar gives a power-up and bonus coins. Tap your coins to open the market and buy power-ups whenever you like.

I left in the middle of a level. Is it lost?

No. Octamine saves an unfinished level after every move. Open the same level again and you'll carry on exactly where you were. On a timed level the clock waits for you.

Settings and your phone

How do I turn off the sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear on the home screen, the level map or during a level. There are sliders for sound effects and music, and a switch for vibration.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Right now your progress is saved on your phone. Cloud save, which will keep your progress when you change phones, is on its way.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling Octamine deletes everything it kept on your phone. For online data, see Your data.
