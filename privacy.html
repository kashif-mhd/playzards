Privacy Policy

For Octamine and this website · Effective: 25 October 2026

The short version

Octamine doesn't ask for your name, email address or an account. Your progress is kept on your phone. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for anything other than running the game.

Who we are

Playzards ("we", "us") is an independent game developer and makes Octamine. You can reach us at playzardsgames@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, Octamine stores these in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your level progress, stars and best results;
  • your coins, power-ups, lives and gift progress;
  • a level you haven't finished yet, so you can carry on later;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration).

This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

What we collect

In the current version of Octamine, we don't collect any information that identifies you.

Advertising

If Octamine shows ads (for example, an optional ad you choose to watch for a reward), they are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Online features (when they're available)

Cloud save and leaderboards are optional. If they're switched on and you use them, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (and, if you sign in, Google Play Games or Game Center) to keep a copy of your progress and to show your leaderboard name and scores to other players. This uses a player ID created for the game, your leaderboard name, your scores and stars, your progress, and your country (for regional leaderboards). See the Unity Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Octamine is a puzzle game for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics, and its fonts are hosted on the site itself. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling Octamine deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • To delete your online data (cloud save and leaderboards), follow the steps on Your data.
  • For anything else, including questions about data held by our service providers, email us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
