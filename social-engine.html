Playzards Social Engine

An internal tool of Playzards · Not available to the public

Uses YouTube API Services

What it is

Playzards is a one-person game studio. We post short gameplay videos of our games to our own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@playzards, and to our other social accounts.

Playzards Social Engine is the small tool we built for that. We write a post once: the clip, its title and its caption. The tool then uploads the video to our channel and reads back how many people watched it, so we can see which clips work. It runs only on the studio's own computer and is used only by Playzards.

The New post page.

What it does with YouTube

  • Signs in to our own Google account with Google's sign-in (OAuth 2.0).
  • Uploads our own videos to our own channel, with the title and description we wrote.
  • Reads the view, like and comment counts of those videos, and our channel's subscriber count.

It doesn't access any other channel or account, show ads, or share YouTube data with anyone. How it handles data is in our Privacy Policy, and using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service (see our Terms).
